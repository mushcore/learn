## Registers are the fastest memory; the PC holds the address of the next instruction

|---|---|---|
| Registers | 1 nsec | < 1 KB |
| Cache | 2 nsec | 4 MB |
| Main memory | 10 nsec | 1 - 8 GB |
| Magnetic disk | 10 msec | 1 - 4 TB |
