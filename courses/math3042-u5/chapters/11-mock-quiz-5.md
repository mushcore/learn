One long quiz over Unit 5: continuous variables, the uniform, normal and exponential distributions, the Z table in every direction, standardizing, percentiles and the R calls. Do it in one sitting with the Z table, a calculator with $e^x$ and $\ln$, and no notes. Every explanation shows the arithmetic; a miss points back to its lesson and returns in the review queue.

:::quiz The paper quiz covers
Part 1, paper and pencil: an area under a uniform density, a Z-table lookup in each direction (left tail, right tail, between, and a percentile read backwards), a normal problem that needs $Z = (X - \mu)/\sigma$ first, and an exponential probability or percentile from $F(x) = 1 - e^{-x/\beta}$. Part 2, RStudio, will follow Lab 6, not yet posted.
:::
